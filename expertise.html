METABOLIC HEALTH & LCHF

A Modern Metabolic Understanding

Dr. Abhijeet has completed a Diploma in LCHF and Metabolic Health, strengthening his clinical focus on dietary patterns, insulin dynamics, and lifestyle-related health concerns.

Metabolic Disorders

Clinical interest in systemic metabolic irregularities including dyslipidemia, high triglycerides, and chronic energy dysregulation. Consultations focus on pinpointing underlying lifestyle imbalances alongside homoeopathic evaluation.

Weight Management

Moving away from arbitrary calorie deprivation toward a physiological understanding of hormones, satiety signals, insulin resistance, and sustainable carbohydrate modification tailored to individual body types.

Diabetes-related Concerns

A focused clinical area evaluating glycemic variability, dietary carbohydrate impacts, lifestyle timing, and concurrent constitutional support without interrupting necessary prescription management.

PCOS / PCOD

Assessing the intricate hormonal-metabolic axis in Polycystic Ovary Syndrome. Consultations address menstrual rhythm, hyperinsulinemia, weight fluctuations, and individual emotional-physical health.

Fatty Liver

Drawing on extensive gastroenterology clinical background, Dr. Abhijeet assists patients with non-alcoholic fatty liver changes through low-glycemic dietary planning and individualized homoeopathic remedies.

Lifestyle-related Concerns

Addressing sleep architecture, chronic mental stress, digestive irregularities, fatigue, and environmental triggers as integral components of the patient's long-term wellness.
