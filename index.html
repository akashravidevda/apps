MEET THE DOCTOR

Clinical Experience. Continuous Learning. Individualized Care.

Dr. Abhijeet A. Gadiwadd (Powar) completed his BHMS degree in 2011 from Gulabrao Patil Homoeopathic Medical College under the Maharashtra University of Health Sciences (MUHS).

Before dedicating himself entirely to Homoeopathy in 2017, he gathered rich and critical clinical exposure in multidisciplinary hospital environments—serving in Gastroenterology, Intensive Care Units (as ICU In-Charge) and specialized Kidney Centres.

This diverse foundation bridges rigorous clinical understanding with gentle, individualized homoeopathic care and evidence-informed metabolic nutrition.
